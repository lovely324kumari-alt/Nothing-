HOW TO PLAY
BASICS
WIZARD CHESS 3D is a complete chess game with standard rules. Select a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a destination square to move it.
PIECE MOVEMENT
Pawn: Moves forward 1 square (2 on first move), captures diagonally.
Rook: Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.
Knight: Moves in an L-shape (2 squares one direction, then 1 perpendicular).
Bishop: Moves any number of squares diagonally.
Queen: Moves any number of squares in any direction.
King: Moves one square in any direction.
SPECIAL MOVES
Castling: King moves 2 squares toward a rook, and the rook jumps to the square the king passed over. Only allowed if neither piece has moved and no pieces are between them.
En Passant: If a pawn moves 2 squares forward from its starting position and lands beside an opponent's pawn, the opponent can capture it as if it had moved only 1 square forward. This must be done on the very next move.
Pawn Promotion: When a pawn reaches the opposite end of the board, it can be promoted to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.
GAME END
Check: When the king is under direct attack. You must get out of check on your next move.
Checkmate: When the king is in check and cannot escape. The game is over.
Stalemate: When a player has no legal moves but is not in check. The game is a draw.
Draw: Can also occur by repetition, insufficient material, or agreement.
CONTROLS
Touch/Mouse: Tap to select, tap again to move.
Camera: Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan.
Buttons: Use the HUD buttons for undo, restart, menu, and camera presets.
